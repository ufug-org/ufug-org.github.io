Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Ufuq Platform, we believe that privacy is a fundamental right, not a luxury. Therefore, all our applications and software are built on the principles of complete transparency and strict commitment to protecting your personal data.

Data Collection and Usage

Our applications do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you or your device. We do not use hidden trackers, and we do not sell any data to third parties. Your activity within our apps remains entirely private and confined to your device.

Local Permissions (e.g., Location Data)

Some of our applications may request specific device permissions to function correctly. For example, our Global Muazzin app requests access to your geographic location solely to calculate accurate prayer times.

We guarantee that this location data is processed locally (offline) on your device. It is NEVER transmitted to our servers, Microsoft servers, or any third-party services.

Transparency and Open Source

To foster trust, we are committed to making most of our software open-source. This means the source code is publicly available for review and auditing by the developer community, ensuring our apps are free from malicious code or secret data-gathering mechanisms.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Some of our apps may offer additional premium features through a subscription model (Freemium). All payment transactions are securely processed exclusively through official storefronts (such as the Microsoft Store). We do not collect, view, or store your credit card details or financial information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, we would be happy to hear from you via our official Telegram channel linked on our platform's homepage.
